## The brand
«دلّة»: fictional gahwa (Arabic coffee) delivery, thermos to your door or office.

## Lesson (the post story, ~8 slides)
1. **Hook:** «كل براندات القهوة العربية شكلها واحد… ليش؟» (brown + gold + calligraphy, a dallah drawing).
2. **Problem:** 4 fictional look-alike logos side by side: you can't tell them apart.
3. **Idea:** the dallah spout bends into the letter «د»; the lid is the dot-like cap. One simple mark, no calligraphy.
4. **Colours:** cardamom green + saffron + cream (no brown): different from the shelf, still warm.
5. **Type:** one Arabic sans + one English, the name in bold.
6. **Result:** cup, thermos, delivery bag, app icon in the profile circle.
7. **Lesson slide:** «اختلف عن الرف: شكل واحد بسيط، ولون محد ماخذه.»
8. **Ask:** «شنو الاسم اللي بعده؟ اكتبه بالكومنت» + save.
