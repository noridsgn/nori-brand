# Concept proposal · Tue 1 Dec 2026 · «سطر»  — ينتظر موافقتك

Fictional brand built as a lesson (problem → idea → result). Not a real brand, no pitch, no DM/price. NOT in Metricool until NORI approves.

**Brand:** «سطر» — a fictional bookshop café (books + coffee, quiet place to read).

## 1. Problem (slide 1–2)
Bookshops all look the same: an open-book icon, brown and beige, an old serif feel. You can't tell one from the other on a street sign or a tote bag.
Slide 2: 4 fictional look-alike bookshop badges (open book, owl, quill, brown) on one shelf.

## 2. Idea (slide 3–4)
Every Arabic letter sits on one line, the baseline (السطر). Every book sits on one line too: the shelf.
The logo = one long line with the word «سطر» sitting on it; the line runs past the word like a shelf with room for more. The line is the whole identity: it underlines prices, divides the menu, runs along the receipt, becomes the bookmark.
Palette (no brown): ink navy, paper white, one signal colour (tomato red) for the line. Type: a calm Arabic sans for text, the logo hand-drawn on the line.

## 3. Result (slide 5–8)
Bookmark (the line becomes the edge), receipt (the line between items), tote bag, the shelf signs inside the shop, the app/profile icon (just the line + «س»), a coffee cup with the line around it.

## 4. Lesson slide (last)
«الفكرة موجودة بالاسم»: 3 steps the designer can try on any name: what does the word mean? what shape lives in that meaning? can one shape carry the whole identity?
Last slide asks to save / follow / comment («شنو اسم براندك؟ شنو الشكل اللي فيه؟»). No DM, no price.
