# Voice reel · Mon 23 Nov 2026 · v2 · الكلام فوق الصورة ما ينقرا

> Replaces plan.md («لا تقول شنو فيه… قول شنو يسوي لك»), which NORI rejected on 23 Nov: marketing (ad copy), not design.
> NORI chose this idea from 3 options (23 Nov). Not scored yet: score with nori-dash scoring/rubric.md before recording.

**Style:** same Adobe-reel method as the original plan: NORI's phone footage of his screen → blurred/darkened motion graphics on each spoken word → back to footage. #0C0C0E + gold #E6B84C, green = good / red = bad, IBM Plex Sans Arabic Bold, rounded caption box above the Instagram buttons, light SFX, closing question with «اكتبها بالتعليقات» button. ~23 s.
**No repeat:** Carousel C (6 Nov) covered font size, contrast and the 3 phone tests (thumb, squint, 2 seconds). This one is only about **text on top of a busy photo** and the 3 ways to fix it. No phone test here.
**Fictional brand:** «فنار» (lighthouse, neutral object name), labelled in the caption.

## Hook (0–1 s)
First frame: a poster for «فنار» with a white headline sitting on a crowded street photo; the words disappear into the photo. First word «شوف» at ~0.2 s.

## Storyboard

| 1 | 0–2.5 | «فنار» poster, white headline lost on a busy street photo | footage → blur on «محد قراه» | red ring on the headline |

## Music & sound
Clean minimal beat, −22 dB. Tick on each «حل», soft whoosh on the gradient and the blur.

## Cover (designed separately)
The lost-headline poster split in half: left lost (red), right fixed (green); text centred «ما ينقرا؟», inside the 3:4 crop.

## Caption (one CTA: comment)
الكلام موجود… بس الصورة أكلته.

3 حلول لما تحط كلام فوق صورة زحمة:
1. حطه بمكان هادي بالصورة
2. تدرج غامق تحته
3. ضبّب ورا الكلام بس

والستروك حول الحروف؟ خله للتسعينات 😅

انت شنو تستخدم؟ اكتبها بالتعليقات 👇🏻

«فنار» براند وهمي سويته للتوضيح.

Let the text breathe.
